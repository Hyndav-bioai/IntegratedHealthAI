Understanding your patterns

Cycle length is measured from the first day of one period to the first day of the next. Estimates become more useful when several start dates are recorded, but they cannot guarantee when your next period will begin.

Cycle changes can have many causes. Seek medical advice for persistent irregularity, unusually heavy bleeding, severe pain or other concerning symptoms. This tracker does not diagnose or replace a clinical assessment.
